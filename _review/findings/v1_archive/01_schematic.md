# Schematic Review

Pre-fabrication review of `twin28xx.kicad_sch` (top), `motor_driver.kicad_sch` (sub, U14/U15), `magnetic_encoder.kicad_sch` (sub, U16/U18). Visuals from `_review/exports/sch_png/sheet_0[1-5].png`. Net topology cross-checked against `twin28xx.kicad_sch` S-expression source.

## BLOCKERS (must fix before fabrication)

- **MT6701 MODE pin (pin 14) is floating** — `magnetic_encoder.kicad_sch:3529..3656` (U16/U18 instance). Visible as unconnected on `sheet_04.png` / `sheet_05.png`. Per MT6701 datasheet, the MODE pin selects output mode: floating/I²C address-program → I²C, GND → ABZ, VDD → UVW, resistor divider → SSI/PWM/analog. The board routes the A/B/Z pins (6/7/8) to MISO/SCK/NSS via 0Ω jumpers R33-R38 (`magnetic_encoder.kicad_sch:2431..2541`), implying SSI use, but with MODE floating the part powers up in I²C mode and pins 6/7/8 act as SDA/SCL/CS — incompatible with the SPI master in firmware. **Recommendation:** tie MODE to the appropriate level (GND for ABZ, or the divider per datasheet for SSI, or VDD for UVW) before fab. This is a population-time fix only if a 0402/0603 footprint exists at the pin; otherwise it is a re-spin.

- **No I²C pull-ups on the QWIIC bus (J5)** — `twin28xx.kicad_sch:21530..21630` (R16=120Ω), R15 = 120Ω (`33012..`), and R18 = 10kΩ (`37748..`) at the J4 jumper. The two 120Ω parts (R15/R16) sit *in series* with I2C_SDA/I2C_SCL on the way to J5; there are no resistors pulling the lines up to +3.3V. R18 (10k) is gated by jumper J4 (`Conn_01x02`, marked DNP / X'd in `sheet_01.png`), and even when fitted it pulls only one line. With no on-board pull-ups and a stand-alone QWIIC peripheral that may not provide its own (most don't on the slave side), the bus will stay floating high-Z and I²C will not function. **Recommendation:** populate J4 (or hard-wire a 4.7kΩ pull-up to +3.3V) on each of SDA and SCL. Confirm the 120Ω series resistors are the intent (they're high for I²C; 22-100Ω is typical for ESD/EMI series) — with 120Ω + ~200pF cable + slave input cap the bus may not meet rise-time spec at 400 kHz.

- **DRV8316 nFAULT / nSLEEP node clamped to ~5.1V and tied directly to 3.3V STM32 GPIOs without a level shifter** — `motor_driver.kicad_sch` (see `s2_drvL.png` crop in `_review/`). On each motor driver, nFAULT (pin 22) and nSLEEP (pin 23) are joined and pulled up by ~100k to **VCC (= +24V battery)** with a BZT52B5V1S 5.1V Zener (D3, D4) clamping the node to 5.1V. The same node connects to STM32 GPIOs (mapped through hierarchical labels nFAULT, nSLEEP — see `motor_driver.kicad_sch:4756, 4811`). STM32G473 GPIOs are nominally 3.3V; FT_a / FT_h pins are 5V-tolerant *only when properly terminated*, and 5.1V at the input exceeds the FT spec margin. nSLEEP is also being driven *by* the MCU (3.3V high) into a node that the Zener wants to keep at 5.1V — the MCU pull-down works (open-drain to GND), but a 3.3V push-pull output high will be sourced into the Zener's reverse current and through the 100k to 24V continuously. **Recommendation:** move the pull-up to +3.3V (replace D3/D4 with a wire link, change the 100k to be from +3.3V instead of VCC), or add a level translator. Without this, the MCU GPIOs are stressed every time nSLEEP is driven and nFAULT readouts may damage the input over time.

## CRITICAL (functional risk, should fix)

- **DRV8316 buck output is the *sole* source of board logic supply when USB is disconnected, and the LDO has marginal headroom at the buck's default voltage.** `twin28xx.kicad_sch:12435` text-box says "BUCK starts at 3.3V default" and "prioritizes VBUS over BUCK"; the topology routes VBUS via D2 (1N4148, ~0.7V drop) and BUCK through Q1 (AO3401A P-FET) into U9 (XC6206P332) Vin (`s1_3v3.png`). XC6206P332 dropout is ~250 mV at 100 mA. With VBUS = 5V we have ~4.3V at LDO Vin → fine. With BUCK at the DRV8316 default of 3.3V, LDO Vin is ~3.3V and Vout will be in dropout (~3.05V). STM32G473 runs fine at 3.0V; **MT6701 spec is 3.3-5V** — operating it at ~3.0V is out of spec. The BOOT0 pull-down is 5.1k, which means the MCU comes up boot-from-flash; if that firmware does not configure DRV8316 BUCK_SEL = 5V over SPI within a few hundred ms, the encoders boot at out-of-spec supply. Also note: at first power-up before any firmware is flashed, the only way to power the +3.3V rail is via USB, because BUCK output won't be reconfigured. **Recommendation:** strap the DRV8316 BUCK_SEL to 5V at hardware (if the part variant supports a strap), or replace XC6206P332 with a wider-input LDO (e.g. AP2112-3.3 with 6V input, or LP5907) that has lower dropout and tolerates 5V comfortably. At minimum, document that the board must be powered via USB *first* on bring-up, before the motor driver is used as the supply source.

- **CAN_VIO is driven by an STM32 GPIO (PC4), not by a power rail.** `twin28xx.kicad_sch:19097, 19407, 19507` (CAN_VIO labels) and the MCU pin map shows PC4/pin 22 = "CAN_VIO" (see `s1_mcu_pinL.png`). CA-IF1044VD-Q1 pin 5 (VIO) needs 1.6-5.5V supply for the digital interface to level-shift correctly. PC4 sourcing 3.3V can supply the small VIO current (~few mA on this part) but: (1) it requires firmware to drive PC4 high before any CAN traffic — if the bootloader or stuck firmware leaves PC4 low, CAN is dead; (2) the GPIO source resistance is ~30Ω at 8 mA, which adds noise margin issues; (3) any bus glitch on VIO can backfeed the GPIO. **Recommendation:** tie VIO directly to +3.3V, or use a load switch fed by +3.3V controlled by PC4 instead of feeding VIO from the GPIO pin.

- **CAN common-mode choke U12 (ACT1210D) is marked DNP / not populated.** Visible as X'd out on `sheet_01.png` (CAN section, `s1_can.png` / `s1_can_full.png`). The footprint is on the board but the part is not populated; CAN_H/CAN_L go straight from U11 to the connectors (J7/J8) and to the U2/U1 mezzanine pins 3/4. Without the choke, common-mode noise from the motor PWM into the CAN cable will degrade communication and may cause EMI emissions failures. The board is intended for a 24V motor controller environment — this is exactly where the choke matters. **Recommendation:** populate U12.

- **CAN bus default termination is OFF** — R21 (120Ω) is at jumper J4 and R27/R28 + C30 split termination is at jumper J6, both jumpers (`Conn_01x02`) are X'd / DNP (`s1_voltdiv.png`, `s1_qwiic2.png`). With no terminator populated by default, a single-board test won't communicate with another node. This is intentional (board is meant as middle node, terminator selectable by jumper), but the silkscreen or assembly notes need to be very clear about this. **Recommendation:** explicit silkscreen guidance ("install JP for 120Ω termination") and verify on first prototypes.

- **No ferrite bead between +3.3V and VDDA** — `twin28xx.kicad_sch:7515..7531` defines the VDDA pin (29). The schematic instance routes VDDA directly off the +3.3V net with C25/C26 (100nF + 1uF) decoupling but no L/ferrite. STM32G473 datasheet recommends ferrite bead + 100nF between VDD and VDDA for ADC accuracy. With current sense feeding ADC1/ADC2/ADC5 (SOA/SOB/SOC for both motors) and ADC sampling at high rates during PWM switching, VDDA noise will couple straight into ADC reading offset. The phase-current-sense topology (DRV8316 internal CS → SOx → 49.9Ω + 10nF RC → ADC) is otherwise reasonable, but the noise floor will be limited by VDDA quality. **Recommendation:** add a ferrite (e.g. BLM18AG601SN1 600Ω@100MHz) between +3.3V and VDDA. If layout does not allow, add a 0Ω jumper now so it can be retrofitted.

- **No fuse / polyfuse on USB VBUS.** `s1_usb_close.png` shows USB-C J2 VBUS goes through SRV05-4A TVS (U6) directly into the +5V tree (D2 → LDO Vin OR-ing). A short on +5V net or LDO failure can sink the full host port current (≤3A on PD-capable hosts) into the board with no protection between the connector and the rail. **Recommendation:** add a 500 mA-1 A polyfuse in series with VBUS at J2 before the SRV05.

- **DRV8316 VREF/ILIM (pin 37) has no decoupling cap to GND.** Per `s2_drvL.png`, pin 37 is tied to +3.3V via R30 = 330Ω with no cap to GND. TI DRV8316 datasheet recommends 100 nF on VREF/ILIM when used as analog reference. **Recommendation:** add a 100 nF NP0 cap from pin 37 to AGND on each of U14 and U15.

- **Motor phase labels PHA1, PHB1, PHC1, PHA2, PHB2 are dangling on the top sheet** — confirmed by `_review/erc.json` (5 ×  `"label_dangling"` errors, severity = error; user-stated "0 violations" was incorrect). The phases come out of `motor_driver.kicad_sch` via hierarchical pins PHA/PHB/PHC and are wired to J9/J10 (3-pin headers) within each sub-sheet, so the motor outputs do go somewhere. The dangling top-sheet labels appear to be unused stubs left over from a redesign; they don't change the netlist (each is on a unique net), but they should be deleted so the ERC report is clean and reviewers don't lose the signal in the noise.

- **DRV8316 datasheet URL is wrong** — `motor_driver.kicad_sch:7188` points to "ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/mct8316z-q1.pdf" (MCT8316Z, a different part family). The component value `DRV8316CRRGFR` is correct in BOM and footprint. Cosmetic but it has misled at least one reviewer (the user) into looking up wrong specs. Same for the second instance (U15 — sheet 3).

## NITS (improvements / cosmetic / minor)

- **HSE crystal load caps are correct, not over-loaded.** User briefing said "C1/C2 = 47pF" — that's wrong. C1/C2 (47pF) are at the USB-C CC1/CC2 footprints and **are marked DNP** (`twin28xx.kicad_sch:21642 (dnp yes)`, `26335 (dnp yes)`). The actual crystal load caps are C11 and C18, both 30pF (0402CG300J500NT, see BOM line 7 and `twin28xx.kicad_sch:31076`). For X322512MSB4SI (CL=20pF) with assumed 5pF stray: required 2×(20-5) = 30pF — exactly matched. Good.

- **DRV8316 DRVOFF (pin 21) is left as no_connect** (`s2_drvL.png` shows the X mark). Per TI datasheet this pin has an internal pull-down (~100k) so floating defaults to driver enabled, which is the desired behavior. Best practice is still to tie it to AGND with a short trace; floating gives slightly more EMI susceptibility. Optional add 0Ω stuff to GND.

- **ADC channel net names don't match STM32 channel numbers.** `s1_mcu_pinR.png`: PA1 (pin 13) is labeled "ADC1_IN3" but the STM32G473 datasheet has PA1 = ADC1_IN2. PA2 (pin 14) is labeled "ADC1_IN4" but it is actually ADC1_IN3. The labels are net names, so the underlying connections are unaffected, but firmware engineers will be confused. Suggest renaming to match ST conventions (or adding a comment in the schematic).

- **GPIO pin assignment quirks:**
  - PB12 (pin 34) = FDCAN_RX is FDCAN2_RX (valid via AF9). PB6 (pin 59) = FDCAN_TX is FDCAN2_TX (valid via AF9). FDCAN1 is *not* used; PA11/PA12 are dedicated to USB DM/DP. This is fine — just note that the FDCAN_TX/RX nets are FDCAN2.
  - PB8 (pin 61) is used as BOOT (with 5.1k pull-down R6 to GND, switch SW1 to +3.3V) and not as FDCAN1_RX. OK because FDCAN1 is unused.
  - PA13/PA14 (SWDIO/SWCLK) brought out to a Tag-Connect TC2030-NL (J3) — good debug access. SWO not exposed.

- **No reset pin cap on actual NRST** — wait, `s1_bootsw.png` and `s1_boot_rst.png` show R7 (10k) pull-up + SW2 + C30 (100nF) on the RESET label which is connected to pin 7 (PG10/NRST). C30 = 100nF is the typical NRST decoupling cap. ✓ Looks fine after closer look. (Removed from earlier draft.)

- **Charge-pump caps on DRV8316 are correct values.** C32/C46 = 47nF (CPH-CPL flying cap), C33/C45 = 1uF (VCP), C31/C47 = 1uF (AVDD), C36/C50 = 100nF + C37-C42 / C51-C56 = 6× 10uF on VM, C43/C57 = 330uF electrolytic. C44/C58 (THT 330uF alternate footprint) marked DNP. All in line with TI EVM reference.

- **Reverse-polarity protection design is unusual but workable.** U4 (WSD4066DN dual N-FET, common-drain, back-to-back) driven by U3 (FMMT620 NPN) with gate bias from D1 + the DRV8316 charge pump (VCP net feeds gate node through 10k). This bootstraps the gate above battery rail for very low Rds(on) ideal-diode behavior. Normal current flow is via the body diode of the second FET while the first FET is fully on, which has the usual low Rds(on) drop. The scheme depends on VCP being available — VCP only ramps up after DRV8316 is enabled, so during the brief startup window the path is through one MOSFET body diode (lossy but functional). Looks reasonable; just verify on the first prototype that VCP successfully drives the FETs hard on.

- **0Ω desolder-jumper topology for onboard vs external encoder is correct** (`magnetic_encoder.kicad_sch` and sheet 4/5 PNGs). R33/R34/R35 (motor 1) and R36/R37/R38 (motor 2) connect U16/U18 A/B/Z pins to MISO/SCK/NSS. J11/J12 (6-pin SH1.0) directly tap the same SPI bus + 3.3V + GND. To use external encoder, populate J11/J12 and *desolder R33-R38* — the schematic comment box explicitly says this. Just be sure to silkscreen this clearly because if both onboard MT6701 and external are populated you get bus contention.

- **CAN-FD transceiver STB pin (pin 8) is correctly tied to GND with EP** (`twin28xx.kicad_sch:14685, 16075` show wires joining pin 8 STB and pin 9 EP, junction at 326.39, 40.64, ground at 326.39, 64.77). Normal-mode operation. ✓

- **USB-C device-mode topology** is correct: R2 (5.1k) on CC1, R3 (5.1k) on CC2 — `twin28xx.kicad_sch:33863, 34044`. SRV05-4A on D+/D- (U6). Note: VBUS has a 0Ω polyfuse footprint absent (see CRITICAL above). Also no series ESD on D+/D- (the SRV05 is shunt-to-rail, which is the standard approach).

- **3.3V LDO output decoupling** (`twin28xx.kicad_sch` near U9): C6 100nF + C7 10uF + C8 10uF — fine for the 200 mA part.

- **Lots of testpoints brought out** for SPI1-SPI4, FDCAN, SOA/SOB/SOC, I2C, BUCK, VCP, VSENS, etc. (TP1..TP46). Excellent debug access. Power LEDs LED1 (VCC) and LED2 (+3.3V) confirm rail status. User LED and 2 fault LEDs (LED4/LED5) on motor drivers. Good.

- **Fiducials FID1/FID2/FID3** — three fiducials present, good for assembly.

- **Mechanical hardware**: H1/H2/H4/H5/H7/H8/H10/H11 = MountingHole_Pad (likely vias), H3/H6/H9/H12 = SMTSOM225BTR (SMT standoffs). Fine.

- **DRV8316 buck inductor L1/L2 = 47uH PRS3015-470MT** — 1A saturation (per part family), DC resistance ~0.4Ω. For the buck's typical 100-200 mA load to logic this is OK, but at peak loads with 5V output the 0.4Ω drops 80 mV at 200 mA — fine. 

- **Aluminum bulk cap C43/C57 = 330uF EEEFT1H331GP** rated 50V. Spec calls for ≥50V which matches. SMD electrolytic with THT alternate footprint (C44/C58, marked DNP) gives manufacturing flexibility. ✓

## Notes

- ERC ran with reasonable severities (`twin28xx.kicad_pro:455-503`); `pin_to_pin` is warning, `pin_not_driven` and `power_pin_not_driven` are error. ERC output (`_review/erc.json`) has 132 entries but only 6 are actual `error`-severity (the dangling phase labels); the remaining 126 are mostly library mismatches (`Symbol 'Test_Point' not found in symbol library 'Mechanical'` × 46), unspecified-pin connection warnings (the CAN driver and STB/EP joining), and a "no connect" flag warning on DRVOFF. None of these (except the dangling labels and the structural items I called out elsewhere) are showstoppers.
- Decoupling counts that *do* check out: STM32 has C20/C21/C22/C23 (100nF × 4) + C24 (4.7uF) on VDD pins (4 VDD pins on LQFP64 = pins 16, 32, 48, 64; counts match). VDDA has C25 (100nF) + C26 (1uF). VBAT (pin 1) has decoupling per the symbol map.
- Phase current sense: per-channel 49.9Ω + 10nF anti-alias filter (R12-R14 + C9/C10/C12 etc.) before STM32 ADC, fc ≈ 320 kHz. Acceptable for ~50 kHz PWM with peak current capture. Good design.
- Power LED current calc: R5 5.1k from VCC (24V) through LED1 = ~4 mA. Within LED Imax. ✓
- Reverse polarity FET WSD4066DN: 30V Vds, ~30 mΩ Rds(on). 3-5A continuous through this is fine (< 0.5W dissipation). For 8A peak the dissipation is ~2W — peak-only is OK.
- BLDC PWM channel routing: TIM1_CH1/CH2/CH3 + complementary CH1N/CH2N/CH3N go to motor 2 INHA/INLA/INHB/INLB/INHC/INLC; TIM8_CH1/CH2/CH3 + complementary go to motor 1. STM32G4 advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8) — both are advanced PWM-capable, suitable for 3-phase BLDC. ✓
- Bottom line: this looks like a well-thought-out design with a couple of bring-up bombs (MT6701 MODE pin floating, no I²C pull-ups, BUCK output level / LDO headroom). The MODE pin and I²C pull-ups are the highest-leverage fixes — a small population change can address each. The level-translation issue on nFAULT/nSLEEP is a re-spin item if true.
